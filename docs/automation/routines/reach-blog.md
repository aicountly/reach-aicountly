# Routine: `reach-blog`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | `reach-blog` |
| **Model** | Claude **Sonnet** (latest Sonnet available to the routine) |
| **Cron** | `30 3-14 * * *` UTC = every hour **09:00–20:00 IST** (12 firings) |
| **Volume** | **one article per firing**; the run stops as soon as today's quota is used (`automation.blog.daily_limit`, default **6/day**) |
| **Session** | fresh session per firing, in the environment that has `aicountly/reach-aicountly` and `aicountly/aicountly-com`; product repos are attached with `add_repo` when needed |
| **Environment** | network access to `reach.aicountly.org` + the Reach network secret (see `../CLAUDE_ROUTINES.md`) — no environment variables needed |

Endpoints (no auth header of your own — the environment's network secret adds `Authorization: Bearer <token>` to every reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/ request):

| Call | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GET  https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/blog-plan` | quota left today, `stop` flag, existing titles/slugs (de-duplication, each with its category and product_slug), priority topics (launch apps + Indian compliance calendar for the next 30 days), internal links; `taxonomy` = the 15 allowed categories and the live / Beta apps; `mix` = last 7 / 30 days by category group and app-led share against the 50/50 target, and `mix.next` = what to write next; `index_suggestions` = the head of the next-topic queue |
| `GET  https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/plan/briefing?type=blog` | read first: today's plan, yesterday vs plan, top 3 lags with causes and what the bot changed, vetting reasons to avoid, topic mix |
| `GET  https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/content-base/next?type=blog` | the next-topic queue in order: roadmap-selected → content-base topics (rewrites come from `regenerate/next`, step 0b), each with the `content_base_key` to submit under |
| `POST https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/blog-drafts` | submit the article; Reach has Gemini vet it, then approves, covers (GPT Image) and publishes it |
| `GET  https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/regenerate/next?type=blog` | a blog whose text failed re-vetting (off-topic, filler, generation meta-text) and was taken down — rewrite it first, same slug |
| `POST https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/content-base/produced` | report the topic you wrote (`outcome: submitted`) or one that does not fit the product (`outcome: not_applicable` + reason) |
| `POST https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/content-base/propose` | optional: add a topic you found (needs evidence) |

The queue lives in Reach's database (not git): the routine never edits files, commits or pushes.
Reach moves the topic from planned → queued → produced → published on its own as the article is
vetted and published.

What happens after the POST is Reach's job, not the routine's: Gemini's decision is final
(approve / correct / reject), the cover image is generated by Reach, and the article goes to
aicountly.com/blogs automatically. The routine does **not** need a second model to fact-check.

## Blog taxonomy (owner decision, 23 Oct 2026)

Every article is filed under exactly **one category slug** and, when it is app-led, the
**product_slug** of the app it is about. Source of truth: `App\Libraries\Blog\BlogTaxonomy`
(served live in `blog-plan` → `taxonomy`); aicountly.com keeps the same list. Any other
category is refused with HTTP 422 listing the allowed slugs.

| Group | `category` | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance | `accounting` | bookkeeping, ledgers, bank reconciliation, month/year-end close, financial statements and reporting |
| Compliance | `gst` | GST returns, ITC, GSTR-2B reconciliation, e-invoicing, e-way bills, rates, registration |
| Compliance | `income-tax` | ITR, advance tax, presumptive tax, deductions, new laws and provisions |
| Compliance | `tds-tcs` | TDS/TCS sections and rates, deposits, 24Q/26Q/27EQ, Form 16/16A, 194Q vs 206C(1H) |
| Compliance | `audit-assurance` | statutory / tax / internal audit, audit trails, audit readiness |
| Compliance | `company-law` | MCA/ROC filings, annual returns, LLP and private company compliance |
| Compliance | `payroll-hr` | payroll, PF, ESI, PT, LWF, payslips, HR compliance |
| Business | `billing-invoicing` | invoices, quotations, credit notes, collections (Billing app articles) |
| Business | `inventory-stock` | stock tracking, warehouses, reorder levels, valuation (stock articles → `inventory-stock` + product_slug `inventory`) |
| Business | `sales-crm` | leads, customers, pipelines, follow-ups |
| Business | `productivity-collaboration` | notes, documents, file storage, sharing, team work (Notes / Drive productivity articles → `productivity-collaboration` + product_slug `notes` / `docs`) |
| Business | `business-operations` | cash flow, funding, subsidies and schemes, processes, growth |
| AICOUNTLY | `product-guides` | how to do one job in an AICOUNTLY app, step by step (always with product_slug) |
| AICOUNTLY | `product-updates` | what shipped recently in an app, from its git log (always with product_slug) |
| AICOUNTLY | `ai-for-finance` | AI in accounting, tax and compliance work — practical, no hype |

`product_slug` (optional) is the aicountly.com catalogue slug of the app the article is
**about**. Live apps: `books` (Smart Books), `billing`, `inventory`, `notes`, `docs` (Drive),
`manage`. Every other product in `taxonomy.products.beta` is **Beta**. Leave product_slug out
of a compliance/finance topic article that only mentions an app in its closing paragraph.

**Mix (owner):** half compliance/finance topic articles, half app-led articles. App-led:
live apps first, about **1 app-led article in 5 about a Beta app**. A Beta app is described
only from its own repository and is always presented as Beta — e.g. "HRMS (Beta)" — and
Reach refuses (422) a Beta product_slug whose article never says "Beta". `blog-plan` →
`mix.next` says what the next article should be: `kind` = `app_led` | `topic` | `either`,
`app_status` = `live` | `beta`, `least_covered_live_apps`, and `why`.

## Prompt

```
You are the AICOUNTLY blog routine. Write and submit exactly ONE marketing/educational
blog article for aicountly.com in this run, then stop.

All Reach calls go to https://reach.aicountly.org/api — no auth header of your own — the environment's network secret adds `Authorization: Bearer <token>` to every reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/ request.

0a. BRIEFING (read it first — this is how the bot learns from its lags)
   GET https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/plan/briefing?type=blog
   - lessons: the top lags of the last 7 days, each with its cause, evidence, guidance and
     what Reach already changed (bot_changed). Follow each lesson's guidance in this run.
   - today: what today's plan expects from this routine (target, achieved_so_far, why).
   - Use avoid_reasons.blog to steer clear of what Gemini rejected recently, and prefer topic_mix.prefer categories (avoid topic_mix.avoid; both are category slugs) when two topics are otherwise equal.
   - topic_mix.steer says which side of the 50/50 app-led/topic mix is behind; blog-plan's
     mix.next (step 1) has the same answer computed live — follow mix.next when they differ.
   - If the call fails, carry on without it — it informs, it never blocks; Reach still
     enforces the quota and Gemini still decides.
0b. REWRITE FIRST
   GET https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/regenerate/next?type=blog
   - If "item" is not null, this run's article is that rewrite: keep item.title's topic and
     item.primary_keyword (you may sharpen the title wording), read item.why for what was wrong
     with the old text and write a completely new article (steps 3–4). Submit it (step 5) with
     "regeneration_id": item.regeneration_id added to the body; Reach keeps the slug, so the
     article returns at the same URL. Skip step 2. A 429 still means stop.
     Reach keeps the rewrite filed where the old article was (its stored category and
     product_slug); send the same category/product_slug you would choose anyway.

1. PLAN
   GET https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/blog-plan
   - If "stop" is true (quota used or automation off): reply "No blog today: <reason>" and end.
   - Read quota, existing_titles, priority_topics, taxonomy, mix, index_suggestions,
     internal_links, rules.
   - mix.next decides the KIND of article for this run: app_led (an article about one app,
     with its product_slug — a live app from mix.next.least_covered_live_apps, or a Beta app
     from taxonomy.products.beta when mix.next.app_status is "beta"), topic (a
     compliance/finance article, no product_slug) or either.

2. CHOOSE ONE TOPIC
   GET https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/content-base/next?type=blog
   Take the FIRST item that you can write truthfully (kind "regeneration", then "roadmap",
   then "content_base" — the order is Reach's priority). Among the roadmap / content_base
   items, prefer the first one whose taxonomy.app_led matches mix.next.kind (true for
   app_led, false for topic; any for either); if none of them matches, take the first one.
   Use its title/brief/details as the brief and submit under its content_base_key EXACTLY
   (it may end in -r2 when an earlier attempt was rejected: status_reason says why — fix
   that). Each item's taxonomy gives the category to file it under (taxonomy.category, when
   set) and, for an app-led topic, the product_slug (taxonomy.product_slug); item.category
   is the portfolio stream, NOT the blog category.
   - If an item is about a product capability that you cannot find in the product's code,
     do not write it: POST https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/content-base/produced
     {"type":"blog","key":"<item key>","outcome":"not_applicable","reason":"<what you
     checked>","routine_run_id":"<this session id>"} and take the next item.
   - Only when the queue is empty (or nothing in it fits), choose your own topic of the kind
     mix.next asks for:
       topic   → a deadline from priority_topics.compliance_calendar in the next 2–3 weeks
                 relevant to Indian small businesses (GST, TDS/TCS, advance tax, PF/ESI, ITR,
                 MCA), else a theme from priority_topics.themes;
       app_led → a practical job one app does — useful advice first, product second: a live
                 app from mix.next.least_covered_live_apps, or, when mix.next.app_status is
                 "beta", a Beta app from taxonomy.products.beta (its repo is listed there);
       either  → whichever of the two gives the better article today.
     Make a content key blog-<YYYYMMDD>-<short-slug> (e.g. blog-20261017-gstr-3b-late-fee).
   The topic must NOT repeat or closely paraphrase anything in existing_titles (same
   question, same angle = duplicate). Never reuse a key from existing_titles.

3. RESEARCH — facts must be real
   Products: if the article mentions any AICOUNTLY app, attach its repository (the "repo"
   field, e.g. aicountly/billing-aicountly) with add_repo, clone it, and read the actual
   code: routes/pages/components/menus, and `git log --since=60.days --oneline` for what
   shipped recently. Only describe features, screens and labels you can see in the code.
   If you cannot confirm a capability, do not claim it. A Beta app (taxonomy.products.beta)
   is described ONLY from its own repository (taxonomy.products.beta[].repo) — never from
   its marketing page or from what a live app does. Write each product claim you rely
   on as one sentence in product_facts with the file it came from, e.g.
   "Billing creates GST invoices with HSN/SAC per line (src/pages/Invoices/InvoiceForm.jsx)".
   Indian law: be exact and current as of today's date (Asia/Kolkata). Cite a section,
   rule, form, notification or rate only when you are certain it is correct and in force.
   The compliance_calendar dates are statutory defaults — extensions by CBIC/CBDT/MCA
   notification happen; if you have web access, check the official source (cbic-gst.gov.in,
   gst.gov.in, incometax.gov.in, mca.gov.in) and list it in sources; if you cannot confirm
   a date, write "the due date is usually the 20th of the following month — check the GST
   portal for any extension" rather than asserting it. No invented statistics, quotes,
   customers or testimonials. No guarantees ("100% compliant", "never miss"). No
   competitor names. General guidance only — suggest confirming specifics with a CA.

4. WRITE (900–1600 words)
   - Indian English, second person, short paragraphs, plain words; explain jargon once.
   - Clean HTML only: <h2>, <h3>, <p>, <ul>, <ol>, <li>, <strong>, <em>, <a>, <blockquote>,
     <table>/<thead>/<tbody>/<tr>/<th>/<td>. No <h1> (the title is rendered separately),
     no images, no inline styles, classes, scripts or iframes.
   - Structure: a 2–3 sentence intro that answers the question, 4–7 <h2> sections, a short
     "Key takeaways" list, and a closing paragraph that mentions the relevant AICOUNTLY app
     naturally (one mention, accurate, no hard sell).
   - Internal links: weave in 2–4 links from internal_links.links (use
     internal_links.site_base + path as absolute https:// URLs) plus, when relevant, the
     launch app's page_url. Descriptive anchor text, never "click here".
   - SEO: meta_title ≤ 60 characters; meta_description 120–160 characters; slug lowercase,
     hyphenated, ≤ 60 characters, no dates unless the article is date-specific; one
     primary_keyword that appears in the title, the first paragraph and one <h2>.
   - summary: 1–2 sentences (≤ 300 characters) for listing cards.
   - cover_prompt: one sentence describing a visual scene for the cover (no text in the
     image), e.g. "a shop owner reviewing a tablet ledger beside a calendar".
   - Beta apps: always present the app as Beta — "<Name> (Beta)" at its first mention and
     wherever you describe what it does; never imply it is generally available.

5. SUBMIT
   POST https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/blog-drafts   Content-Type: application/json
   {
     "content_base_key": "blog-20261017-...",
     "title": "...",
     "summary": "...",
     "body_html": "<p>...</p>...",
     "seo": {"slug": "...", "meta_title": "...", "meta_description": "...", "primary_keyword": "..."},
     "category": "<one slug: accounting | gst | income-tax | tds-tcs | audit-assurance | company-law | payroll-hr | billing-invoicing | inventory-stock | sales-crm | productivity-collaboration | business-operations | product-guides | product-updates | ai-for-finance>",
     "product_slug": "<app-led only: books | billing | inventory | notes | docs | manage | a Beta slug from taxonomy.products.beta>",   (omit for a topic article)
     "tags": ["...", "..."],
     "portfolio_stream": "marketing" | "product" | "problem_to_product",
     "funnel_stage": "top" | "middle" | "bottom",
     "search_intent": "informational" | "commercial",
     "product_facts": ["<fact> (<file>)", ...],
     "sources": ["https://...", ...],
     "cover_prompt": "...",
     "provenance": {"generated_by_model": "claude-sonnet", "routine_run_id": "<this session id>"}
   }
   content_base_key = the queue item's content_base_key when you took one from the queue.
   category = the slug from the taxonomy table above (the queue item's taxonomy.category when
   it has one); product_slug = the app the article is about, only for an app-led article.

6. READ THE RESPONSE
   - 201 / 200: report  "<status> — <title> (<slug>) — vetting: <pipeline.status_label>"
     pipeline.status is one of: live, publishing, approved, awaiting_cover,
     pending_vetting (Gemini unavailable — Reach retries on its own), held_high_risk,
     rejected (pipeline.reasons says why — note them for next time; do not resubmit the
     same article).
   - "already_ingested": this key was used before — pick a different topic and key once.
   - 409 duplicate topic: choose a genuinely different topic once and submit again.
   - 422: fix exactly what the error says and resubmit once (an unknown category or
     product_slug answer lists the allowed values; a Beta app must be presented as Beta).
   - 429 (quota used) or 403 (automation switched off): stop immediately.
   Never submit more than one new article per run.

7. REPORT THE TOPIC (after a 201 or 200)
   If the article came from the queue: POST
   https://reach.aicountly.org/api/v1/automation/content-base/produced
   {"type":"blog","key":"<item key>","outcome":"submitted","content_base_key":"<the key you
   submitted under>","routine_run_id":"<this session id>"}. The answer's status (queued /
   produced / published) is Reach's own record — report it as given.
   If you wrote your own topic and the queue had fewer than 3 items, you may also propose up
   to 3 future topics you came across: POST …/content-base/propose {"type":"blog","items":
   [{"key","title","brief","portfolio_stream","topic_category" (a category slug),"product_slug" (app-led only),"seed_keyword","target_date","evidence":[{"repo",
   "commits","file","note"} or {"url":"https://<official source>","note"}]}],"routine_run_id"}.
   Only topics grounded in code you read or an official source.
```
